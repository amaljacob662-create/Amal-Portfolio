Audio Experience — UX Research

  • UX Research, Interviews, Surveys
  • Affinity Mapping
  • 2025

Project info

Focus
User research & insights
Methods
Interviews, survey, affinity mapping
Year
2025
Tools
Miro

01 — Overview

Understand user behaviors and needs through both qualitative and quantitative data, and identify opportunities to improve how people discover, organize, and share audio content.

02 — The problem

People use multiple platforms to listen to music, podcasts, and other audio, but these services don’t fully support real user needs. The experience is often fragmented, overly algorithm-driven, and lacks emotional and social depth.

  • Audio content spread across multiple platforms
  • Generic recommendations that ignore context and mood
  • Impersonal and limited sharing features
  • Lack of clear structure and navigation

03 — Research and insights

A research-driven, user-centered approach combining qualitative interviews and quantitative survey data. Insights were structured through affinity mapping in Miro to identify patterns and key themes.

04 — Design process

  • User interviews (qualitative) + survey (quantitative)
  • Data breakdown into categories
  • Clustering in Miro
  • Identifying patterns
  • Defining pain points
  • Creating HMW questions
  • Ideation

05 — Final solution

Problem 1: Fragmented experience → A unified platform that gathers all audio content in one place

Problem 2: Weak personalization → Smarter recommendations based on mood, context, and user input, combined with human curation

Clear insights into user needs, behaviors, and frustrations, leading to concepts for a more unified, personalized, and socially engaging audio experience.

06 — Reflection

  • Combining qualitative and quantitative data provides a deeper understanding
  • User behavior is strongly influenced by context, mood, and daily routines
  • Algorithms alone are not enough—users value control and human input
  • Structured research helps turn complex data into clear design opportunities
