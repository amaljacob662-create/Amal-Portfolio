Canopy — UX/UI Design

  • UX Design, Visual Design, Prototyping
  • At WebRay Studio
  • 2026

Project info

Canopy is a website design project for a Mac writing app created for indie developers and writers. The goal was to create a warm, minimal, and distraction-free visual experience that reflects the app’s focus on writing. The design combines a clean layout with thoughtful details to give the product a distinct personality while keeping the experience simple and clear.

My role
UX/UI Designer Intern
Context
Internship project at WebRay Studio
Year
2026
Tools
Figma

01 — Overview

Design a clean, modern website for Canopy that communicates the app’s value, creates a calm writing-focused atmosphere, and presents the product in a visually engaging way.

02 — The problem

Writing tools need to help users focus without adding unnecessary distractions. A website that feels too plain or generic can also struggle to communicate the product’s personality and value.

  • Creating a visual identity that feels unique and memorable
  • Balancing minimalism with warmth and personality
  • Making the hero section visually engaging without overwhelming users
  • Creating a clear visual hierarchy and guiding users toward key actions

03 — Research and insights

The design uses a warm, minimalist visual style inspired by the simplicity of Mac applications. A soft neutral palette with forest green accents helps create a calm and focused atmosphere. Typography, spacing, shadows, and the Mac window were refined to give the hero section more depth while keeping the overall design clean.

04 — Design process

Started by exploring visual directions through moodboards and developing design ideas in Figma. Created wireframes and UI concepts, explored multiple hero section variations, and refined the design through feedback from the WebRay Studio team and several iterations. Each round focused on improving the visual hierarchy, adding personality, and creating a more cohesive design.

05 — Final solution

Problem 1: A visual design that felt too plain → Introduced a soft green background, layered shadows, and a more prominent Mac window to add depth and character.

Problem 2: A lack of clear visual hierarchy → Refined the headline, navigation, spacing, and CTA buttons to make the page easier to scan and the key elements more noticeable.

A more polished and distinctive website design for Canopy, with a stronger hero section, clearer visual hierarchy, and a warmer visual identity. The final direction balances minimalism with depth and personality while keeping the product at the center of the experience.

06 — Reflection

  • Minimal design still needs personality, depth, and visual contrast
  • Feedback helps identify areas that are difficult to notice when working independently
  • Exploring multiple design directions can lead to stronger solutions
  • Small details, such as shadows, spacing, and typography, can make a meaningful difference
  • A good design balances visual appeal with clarity and usability
